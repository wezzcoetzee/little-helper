# little-helper

## Register

product

## Users
Wesley and people who want fewer extensions while inspecting websites, reading GitHub, and clipping content for AI tools.

## Product Purpose
One Chrome extension combining browser utilities from SuperLevels, GitHub media and reading tools, and SnipSnapp Markdown capture.

## Brand Personality
Direct, quiet, useful.

## Anti-references
Decorative cards, dense emoji navigation, looping motion, marketing copy, and unnecessary configuration.

## Design Principles
- Current-site actions first, browser-wide tools one tab away.
- Preserve useful capabilities without duplicate controls.
- Keep longer editing workflows in a side panel.
- Explicitly enable automatic tab closing and audio capture.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, labelled inputs, readable contrast, and no continuous animation.
